# ListItem

A list row of at least 64px, with a leading element, a title, a description of two lines at most, and a chevron when the row opens a detail.

## When to use it

For lists of members, exchanges, notifications, settings. Rows go in a `ul`, usually inside a flush `Card`; a `border-default` separator divides them.

## Parts

```tsx
<ListItem>
  <Avatar name="Jane Doe" decorative />
  <ListItemContent>
    <ListItemTitle>
      <ListItemLink href="…">Jane Doe</ListItemLink>
    </ListItemTitle>
    <ListItemDescription>Description</ListItemDescription>
  </ListItemContent>
  <ListItemChevron />
</ListItem>
```

- `ListItem` (required): the row, an `li`.
- A leading `Avatar` or `Icon`, first in the row.
- `ListItemContent` (required): the title and the description, which take the remaining width.
- `ListItemTitle` (required), `ListItemDescription`: two lines at most.
- `ListItemHeader`: the `ListItemTitle`, then a badge, an amount or a date on the title's line. The description below keeps the full width, even on a phone.
- `ListItemChevron`: shows that the row opens a detail.
- `ListItemLink` or `ListItemButton`, inside the `ListItemTitle`: the whole row becomes a link or a button, named by the title, which covers the row. `ListItemLink` takes the router's link component as `Link` (an `<a>` by default).
