# ListItem

A list row of at least 64px, with a leading element, a title, a description of two lines at most and a trailing element (metadata or chevron).

## When to use it

For lists of members, exchanges, notifications, settings. Rows go in a `ul`, usually inside a flush `Card`; a `border-default` separator divides them.

## What the consumer provides

- `title`, `description`.
- `leading`: an `Avatar` or an `Icon`.
- `trailing`: badge, amount, date.
- `chevron`: shows that the row opens a detail.
- `href` or `onClick` make the whole row clickable: the title becomes the link or button, and covers the row.
