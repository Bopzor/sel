# ListItem

A list row of at least 64px, with a leading element, a title, a description of two lines at most, and a chevron when the row opens a detail.

## When to use it

For lists of members, exchanges, notifications, settings. Rows go in a `ul`, usually inside a flush `Card`; a `border-default` separator divides them.

## Parts

```tsx
<ListItem.Root>
  <Avatar name="Jane Doe" decorative />
  <ListItem.Content>
    <ListItem.Title>
      <ListItem.Link href="…">Jane Doe</ListItem.Link>
    </ListItem.Title>
    <ListItem.Description>Description</ListItem.Description>
  </ListItem.Content>
  <ListItem.Chevron />
</ListItem.Root>
```

- `ListItem.Root` (required): the row, an `li`.
- A leading `Avatar` or `Icon`, first in the row.
- `ListItem.Content` (required): the title and the description, which take the remaining width.
- `ListItem.Title` (required), `ListItem.Description`: two lines at most.
- `ListItem.Header`: the `ListItem.Title`, then a badge, an amount or a date on the title's line. The description below keeps the full width, even on a phone.
- `ListItem.Chevron`: shows that the row opens a detail.
- `ListItem.Link` or `ListItem.Button`, inside the `ListItem.Title`: the whole row becomes a link or a button, named by the title, which covers the row. `ListItem.Link` takes the router's link component as `Link` (an `<a>` by default).
