# Card

A rounded surface that groups content, with an optional title, header action and footer actions.

## When to use it

- For a post in a feed, a profile block, a summary (balance).
- With `href` or `onClick`, the whole card becomes clickable (`shadow-md` on hover, focus ring): the title becomes the link or button, and covers the card. In that case, a title is required and there are no buttons inside.
- `flush` removes the padding, to hold a list (`ListItem`).

## What the consumer provides

- `title`, `subtitle` (author, date), `headingLevel` (2, 3 or 4; 3 by default, to adjust to the page hierarchy).
- `action`: a badge or an icon button at the top right.
- `footer`: one or two buttons.
- `children`: the content.

Do not nest cards.
