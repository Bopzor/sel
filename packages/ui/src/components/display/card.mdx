# Card

A rounded surface that groups content, with an optional header, body and footer.

## When to use it

- For a post in a feed, a profile block, a summary (balance).
- To hold a list of `ListItem`s, from edge to edge.
- Clickable as a whole, to open a detail (`shadow-md` on hover, focus ring).

## Parts

```tsx
<Card>
  <CardHeader>
    <CardTitle>Title</CardTitle>
    <CardDescription>Author · Date</CardDescription>
    <CardAction><Badge>Category</Badge></CardAction>
  </CardHeader>
  <CardBody>…</CardBody>
  <CardFooter>…</CardFooter>
</Card>
```

- `Card` (required): the surface. Its parts pad themselves; content placed directly in it, such as a `ul` of `ListItem`s, goes from edge to edge.
- `CardHeader`: the title, the description and the action.
- `CardTitle`: the title, a heading of `level` 2, 3 or 4 (3 by default, to adjust to the page hierarchy).
- `CardDescription`: under the title: author, date.
- `CardAction`: a badge or an icon button at the top right, written after the title and the description.
- `CardBody`: the content.
- `CardFooter`: one or two buttons.
- `CardLink` or `CardButton`, inside the `CardTitle`: the whole card becomes a link or a button, named by the title, which covers the card. The `CardAction` and the `CardFooter` stay above it, so that their buttons keep their own action.

Do not nest cards.
