# SideNav

The side navigation on desktop, with the instance's logo and name at the top and sections of entries.

## When to use it

From 1024px, as the main navigation. It shows every section, grouped by theme, where `BottomNav` only shows four plus "More".

## Growing the navigation

Entries are arranged in stable groups, for example "Exchange" (Requests, Loans, Calendar), "Community" (Members, Information) and "My account". A new feature joins the group it belongs to rather than creating a new one. Beyond seven entries in a group, the grouping is reviewed.

## Parts

```tsx
<SideNav aria-label="Main navigation">
  <SideNavHeader logo={logo} name="Instance name" place="Geographical area" />
  <SideNavSection>
    <SideNavItem href="/" icon="home" active>
      Home
    </SideNavItem>
  </SideNavSection>
  <SideNavSection title="Community">…</SideNavSection>
  <SideNavFooter>
    <SideNavSection>…</SideNavSection>
  </SideNavFooter>
</SideNav>
```

- `SideNav` (required): the `<nav>`, named by its `aria-label` ("Main navigation").
- `SideNavHeader`: the instance's identity, read from its configuration. `logo` is the URL of a square image, shown at `size-logo` (44px) with `radius-md` corners, next to the `name` and the `place`.
- `SideNavSection`: a group of entries with its `title`. The first section, the main entries, has no title.
- `SideNavItem`: an entry, a link with its `icon` and its label. `active` marks the entry of the current page with `aria-current="page"`. `badge`: a `Badge` after the label, such as a count.
- `SideNavFooter`: at the bottom of the bar, the member's account and signing out, in a `SideNavSection`.

Width `width-sidebar` (280px). Entries are `control-md` (48px) high with `radius-md` corners. Active entry: `bg-primary-subtle` background, `text-primary` text and icon. The application places the bar (`className="sticky top-0 h-dvh"`, for example) and hides it below `lg`.
