# RadioGroup, Radio, RadioCard

The options of a single choice: a `RadioGroup` of `Radio`s (a simple list) or of `RadioCard`s (large cards with a description). The group has no question nor error: they go on a fieldset around it.

## When to use it

- For a single choice among two to five options, all visible.
- `RadioCard` for a structuring choice that needs an explanation (post type, exchange mode): each option becomes a large hit area.

## What the consumer provides

- `RadioGroup`:
  - `value` + `onChange(value)` for a controlled group, `defaultValue` for an uncontrolled one; the value is `null` while no option is chosen. With react-hook-form, the group goes through a `Controller`.
  - `name`, `form`, `required`, `disabled`, and the HTML props of `<div>`.
  - `aria-invalid`: marks every option as invalid; the error message goes on the fieldset around the group.
  - Outside a fieldset, an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`: the group has no name of its own. Inside Ark's `Fieldset`, the legend names it, and the fieldset's `disabled` and `invalid` states apply to it; the group's own props override them.
  - `className`: the options are stacked with `gap-4`; use `gap-3` for cards.
- `Radio` and `RadioCard`: `value`, `label`, `description` (a consequence or a detail, in `text-muted`, linked with `aria-describedby` rather than part of the name), `disabled`.

Offer a default option when one answer is clearly the most common.

The circles are 24px, the hit area 44px high. A chosen card has a `border-primary` border thickened from the inside and a `bg-primary-subtle` background.
