# Fieldset

A group of controls that answer one question, with the question, a hint and an error: the options of a `RadioGroup`, one or several `Checkbox`es.

## When to use it

Around every `RadioGroup` of a form, and around checkboxes that need an error ("Accept the terms to continue"). A single control with a label of its own goes in a `Field` instead.

## Parts

```tsx
<Fieldset invalid={invalid}>
  <FieldsetHeader>
    <FieldsetLegend>Question</FieldsetLegend>
    <FieldsetHint>Hint</FieldsetHint>
  </FieldsetHeader>
  <RadioGroup>…</RadioGroup>
  <FieldsetError>Error</FieldsetError>
</Fieldset>
```

- `Fieldset` (required): the `<fieldset>`. `invalid` shows the error, `disabled` disables every control inside.
- `FieldsetLegend` (required): the question, which names the group.
- `FieldsetHeader`: groups the legend and the hint, closer to each other than to the controls. Only needed with a hint.
- `FieldsetHint`: help shown under the question, linked to the group with `aria-describedby`.
- `FieldsetError`: what to do to fix the answer, only rendered while the fieldset is `invalid`, announced when it appears.

## Rules

- Ark's `Fieldset` names the group with the legend and passes its `disabled` and `invalid` states to a `RadioGroup` inside: they are set on the `Fieldset`. A `Checkbox` inside still takes its own `aria-invalid`.
- Validate on submit, never on every change.
