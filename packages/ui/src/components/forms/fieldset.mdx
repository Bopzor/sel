# Fieldset

A group of controls that answer one question, with the question, a hint and an error: the options of a `RadioGroup`, one or several `Checkbox`es.

## When to use it

Around every `RadioGroup` of a form, and around checkboxes that need an error ("Accept the terms to continue"). A single control with a label of its own goes in a `FormField` instead.

## Parts

```tsx
<Fieldset.Root invalid={invalid}>
  <Fieldset.Header>
    <Fieldset.Legend>Question</Fieldset.Legend>
    <Fieldset.Hint>Hint</Fieldset.Hint>
  </Fieldset.Header>
  <RadioGroup.Root>…</RadioGroup.Root>
  <Fieldset.Error>Error</Fieldset.Error>
</Fieldset.Root>
```

- `Fieldset.Root` (required): the `<fieldset>`. `invalid` shows the error, `disabled` disables every control inside.
- `Fieldset.Legend` (required): the question, which names the group.
- `Fieldset.Header`: groups the legend and the hint, closer to each other than to the controls. Only needed with a hint.
- `Fieldset.Hint`: help shown under the question, linked to the group with `aria-describedby`.
- `Fieldset.Error`: what to do to fix the answer, only rendered while the fieldset is `invalid`, announced when it appears.

## Rules

- Ark's `Fieldset` names the group with the legend and passes its `disabled` and `invalid` states to a `RadioGroup` inside: they are set on the `Fieldset`. A `Checkbox` inside still takes its own `invalid`.
- Validate on submit first, then on each change.
