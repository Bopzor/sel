# Toast

A short, temporary notification at the bottom of the screen, to confirm an action.

## When to use it

- To confirm an action ("Request posted"), including when it leads to another page: the toast container lives at the root of the application and survives navigation.
- Never for a blocking error or information to remember (deadline, next step): use `Alert`.

## What the consumer provides

- `children`: three to six words.
- `tone`: `success` (default), `info`, `danger`.
- `onClose` + `closeLabel`: a close button, named by `closeLabel`.

## Rules

`Toast` is only the rendering. The application's toast library shows, queues, places and dismisses the toasts, and announces them to screen readers from its live region; it is configured to follow these rules:

- Shown for 6 seconds minimum; the timer pauses on hover or focus.
- Position: centered at the bottom on mobile, above the navigation bar; bottom right on desktop. One toast at a time.
