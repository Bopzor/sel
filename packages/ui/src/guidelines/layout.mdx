# Layout

Mobile first, from a 360px screen, then enriched at each breakpoint.

## Breakpoints

| Breakpoint   | Width         | Navigation  | Margins | Columns                            |
| ------------ | ------------- | ----------- | ------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Mobile       | \< 640px      | `BottomNav` | `px-4`  | 1                                  |
| Large mobile | ≥ `sm` 640px  | `BottomNav` | `px-4`  | 1                                  |
| Tablet       | ≥ `md` 768px  | `BottomNav` | `px-6`  | 1 to 2                             |
| Desktop      | ≥ `lg` 1024px | `SideNav`   | `px-10` | 2 to 3                             |
| Large screen | ≥ `xl` 1280px | `SideNav`   | `px-10` | centered content, `max-width-page` |

## Structure of a screen

- **Header**: page title (`title-1`), back button on the left on detail screens, one or two actions on the right.
- **Content**: a reading column of `max-width-content` (720px) at most; forms never go wider, even on large screens.
- **Main action**: on mobile, at the bottom of the screen, full width (`<Button size="lg" className="w-full">`), above the navigation bar; on desktop, at the end of the form, aligned right.
- **Navigation**: see below.

## Navigation

The navigation will evolve with new features (a section dedicated to loans, for example). It is designed to grow without being reorganized at each addition:

- A single list of entries, defined in the application configuration, feeds `BottomNav` and `SideNav`. Each entry has a concept (for its icon), a one-word label, a group and a priority.
- On mobile, `BottomNav` shows the four highest-priority entries and a "More" entry that opens the full list. On desktop, `SideNav` shows everything, by group.
- An instance can disable a feature: its entry disappears from both navigations with no other change.
- Profile, settings and sign-out stay grouped under "My account".

## Safe areas

On phones, the bottom bar and the sheets add `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. The content reserves `spacing-bottom-nav` at the bottom so it never goes under the bar.

## Density

A single, comfortable density. Desktop gains columns, not smaller elements; the `sm` control size is reserved for administration areas.

## Lists and feeds

- Posts are shown as `Card`s in one column on mobile, in a grid of two or three columns from 1024px.
- Lists of members and exchanges use `ListItem` in a flush `Card`.
- Loading: a `Skeleton` shaped like the content, then the content; pagination with a "Show more" button rather than infinite scroll, to keep a landmark.
