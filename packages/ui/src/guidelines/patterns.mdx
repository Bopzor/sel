# Common flows

A few situations come up everywhere. They are always built the same way, so members find their bearings from one screen to the next.

## Filling a form

- One field per row, in the order the questions come to mind. Optional fields are marked "(optional)".
- More than six fields: split into steps with `Stepper`, and keep the answers when going back.
- The submit button stays enabled, even when the form is incomplete: disabled, it does not say what is missing. Validation happens on submit first, then on each change of the fields. `disabled` is kept for actions that cannot be performed at all, with the reason shown next to them.
- On submit, if errors remain: a `danger` `Alert` at the top that lists them with a link to each field, and the focus moved to this alert.
- After submitting: a `success` `Toast`, whether the member stays on the page or lands on the created content. The toast container is mounted at the root of the application, above the router, so the message survives the page change.
- A `success` `Alert` is reserved for confirmations that must stay in view because they contain information to remember or a next step ("Your registration has been sent. A coordinator will approve it within 48 hours").

## Sending or requesting units

1. Choose the member (search with `<Input icon="search">`, results as `ListItem`s with an `Avatar`).
2. Enter the amount (`Input` with `inputMode="numeric"` and a `suffix`) and the reason.
3. Review: a `Dialog` that repeats the member, the amount, the reason and the new balance, with "Send 20 units" and "Cancel".
4. Confirmation: back on the exchange page with a `success` `Toast` ("20 units sent to Lucas Petit"); the balance shown with the `amount` style is already up to date.

## Deleting or declining

- Reversible: the action happens immediately, a `Toast` offers "Undo" for 10 seconds.
- Irreversible: an `alert` `Dialog` with a title that names the object ("Delete the request “Help with a move”?") and a `danger` button.

## Waiting

- Less than 300 ms: nothing.
- Loading a list or a page: `Skeleton`.
- Action started by a button: `loading` on that button.
- Beyond 10 seconds: a message that says what is happening and offers to retry.

## Failing

- Offline: a dedicated screen replaces the page content (built like an `EmptyState`: title "You are offline", explanation, "Retry" button). The navigation stays visible. Actions are not queued; a form in progress keeps what was typed so it can be sent again once the connection is back.
- Server error: a `danger` `Alert` with "Retry", never a technical code.

## Discovering

The first sign-in presents the essentials in three screens at most (what the application is for, how to post a request, how exchanges work), always skippable. `EmptyState`s then guide each empty section.
